The landing page's main navigation (`site/index.html`, `#main-nav`) holds Guide, Design,
Reference, Roadmap and GitHub. The FAQ, and the Diagnostics page, are linked only from the
footer. A reader who starts from the questions scrolls the whole landing page to find them.

Sessions: `r01-faq-reader` at every viewport. The FAQ step costs 23 wheel turns at
390x844, 13 at 1440x900 and 1440x900-like widths (14 at 1280x800, 15 at 1024x768, 13 at
768x1024). It sets the round's missed `trial.step_actions.max` bar at both gating viewports
(23 and 13 against at most 2) and the ratchet's `faq-platforms` `screen_only.steers` (12 and 22).

Cost: S. One line: put FAQ in the main navigation (and the narrow-screen menu).
