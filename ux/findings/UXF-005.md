At 390x844 the quickstart's steps took the width of their longest command line, so the
whole page was 618 px wide; the example window's tab row grew past the window, which clips
it, so the third tab ("session") was cut off; and at every width a milestone with an inline
`code` element put that element into the 5rem tag column, where it ran past its box.

Before: `layout.overflow` 10 at 390x844, 2 at 1440x900, and the hero's text counted as
truncated.

Fix: `site/assets/site.css`: the steps grid is `minmax(0, 1fr)` and long paths in step text
break; the tab row may shrink and wraps. `site/index.html`: each milestone's text is one
`span`, so it stays in the second column.

After: `layout.overflow` 0 at both gating viewports.
