In the landing page's light theme the section kickers, the "next" milestone tag and the
group headings of the build-speed table use the accent `#e85d00` as text colour: 3.23 to 3.5
against their backgrounds, below the 4.5:1 that text of that size needs.

Before: 16 nodes at both viewports in the light theme, then 4 more (the build-speed table
headings) once the first ones were fixed.

Fix: `site/assets/site.css`: a text accent `--accent-text`, `#b23c00` in the light theme
(5.8:1 on `#fffaf4`) and unchanged in the dark one, for `.kicker`, `.tag-next` and
`.speed-table .group th`.

After: 0 nodes.
