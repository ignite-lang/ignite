mdBook's `#mdbook-sidebar-toggle` is a `<label>` carrying `aria-label`, `aria-expanded` and
`aria-controls`: a label has no role that allows them, so assistive technology does not
announce it as a toggle, and it takes no focus, so a keyboard user on a narrow screen cannot
open the table of contents.

Before: 360 axe nodes (one critical, one serious per book page, viewport and theme).

Fix: `site/book/theme/index.hbs` gives the label `role="button"` and `tabindex="0"`;
`site/book/theme/ignite.js` makes Enter and Space click it.

After: 0 nodes; the screen-only user's keyboard runs at 390x844 can reach the toggle.
