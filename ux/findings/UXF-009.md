Where the book's sidebar is open by default (1280 px and wider), it comes first in the
document (`site/book/theme/index.hbs`: `#mdbook-sidebar` before `#mdbook-menu-bar`) and the
book has no skip link (the landing page has one). The first Tab lands on "Welcome"
(`review-book-first-tab-1440x900.png`), and every sidebar entry, 50 and more, stands between
the reader and the search button, the "Ignite home" link and the page's own content.

Sessions: `anchor-keyboard-reference-1440x900` and `-1280x800`: 435 actions for three tasks,
against 167 to 180 at 390x844, 768x1024 and 1024x768, where the sidebar starts closed and
"Ignite home" is 4 Tabs away. The pointer card `anchor-newcomer` does the same kind of
journey in 8 actions. Ratchet at 1440x900: `keyboard.actions` 72 (std-fs), 76
(http-example), 66 (diagnostic-search) against 43, 53 and 16 at 390x844. Every wrong turn
the keyboard reader takes into the book costs 57 Tabs to undo.

Cost: S. One line: a skip link to the menu bar or content on book pages, or the menu bar
before the sidebar in the tab order.

Fix: the book template (`site/book/theme/index.hbs`) puts `#mdbook-sidebar` after the page wrapper, so the menu bar comes first in the tab order; the sidebar stays at the side (it is positioned by CSS, and its rules select it as a sibling of the toggle anchor, which still precedes it). The script that sets the sidebar's ARIA state moved after it. At 1440x900 keyboard.actions: std-fs 72 to 40, http-example 76 to 50, diagnostic-search 66 to 14.
