# Approval: wave 11, every quality row back at or better than v0.15.0 before v0.16.0 is cut

## Words

- Timo, 2026-11-10, after the dedicated-host run of `4520b8cf` was refused and the previous
  coordinator proposed to accept a build time 21 % above v0.15.0: "Okay, now make sure we can
  bring back our quality numbers and improve, actually every release should be an improvement in
  quality, not even allowed to accept 10% degregation."
- Timo, 2026-11-10: "you must check the results which were run on the remote EC2 - not local,
  right".
- Timo, 2026-11-10, asked whether rows 1.6 (units the evaluator cannot run, 236 at v0.15.0, 362
  now) and 3.6 (`unsafe` sites, 12 / 82 at v0.15.0, 25 / 95 now) block v0.16.0 until they are
  back: "A" (both block).
- Standing: `approval-record:standing-waves-2026-11-10` ("i approve all future 10 waves"; the
  stage-1 stop is waived, the page is still written, a release follows each wave).

## Decision

- v0.16.0 is not cut until no row of `docs/quality.md` is worse than v0.15.0: timing and
  benchmark rows on the dedicated host's records, count rows against `quality/baseline.json` at
  the tag `v0.15.0`. The rule is in `AGENTS.md` § Releases.
- Nine stories are accepted for wave 11, one per group of rows that is worse. Inferred by the
  coordinator, not said: "make sure we can bring back our quality numbers" is read as accepting
  the stories that bring them back; which stories those are follows from six diagnoses
  (`.engineering/waves/2026-11-11-wave-11-diagnosis/`).
- `story:remote-refusals-honour-decisions` (draft) asked that the dedicated-host check honour a
  recorded decision. The first sentence above rules that out; it is archived and
  `story:release-refuses-every-worse-row` supersedes it.
- `story:tls-engine-own-crates` and `story:typer-record-literal-deferred` stay drafts until the
  wave closes: their outcomes are acceptance lines of `story:binary-size-back` and
  `story:ignored-tests-and-long-files-back`.
- At most two implementors run at once, model opus, as in the words recorded in
  `approval-record:wave-10-2026-11-10`.
- Integration branch: `wave/10-tls` in the release tree, which holds waves 8 to 10 and the
  v0.16.0 release commits and is not pushed. One pull request brings it to `main` after a green
  `task gate` and a dedicated-host run with no refusal.
- Commits authorised: the opening store commit, the uncommitted dev-profile change of the
  release tree as a commit of its own, the unit commits, their merges, the coordinator's
  integration commits (`CODEGEN_REVISION`, `quality/baseline.json` blesses and withdrawn
  decisions, `docs/slice.md` numbering), the quality records, the closing store commit, the pull
  request merge, then the release of `AGENTS.md` § Releases.

## Decisions the coordinator took, open to Timo's word

- The LLVM target's release runtime archive is built on a standard library without its
  backtrace symbolizer, which needs an unstable cargo flag on the stable toolchain and the
  standard library's source where the archive is built. Reason: the two LLVM cells of row 3.9
  stop 5 to 6 % above v0.15.0 with the scheduler linked on a stock standard library (measured);
  with this they are 11 and 19 % below. The other measured way is a size rewrite of the
  scheduler, which is larger and unmeasured.
- A container's CPU quota stays honoured and `IGNITE_IO=threads` stays, although removing either
  saves bytes.
- Row 3.6 on the Rust target needs three removals on request paths (the allocator's `realloc`
  override, a byte search, a directory read). They are taken; the dedicated-host run decides
  whether they stay.
