What held:
- Byte order across the handover: one `unsent` buffer, and attach sends SUBs and held messages first.
- No chunk is sent twice; an old writer has its own stream and its `Lost` is filtered by generation.
- No backlog counter that can drift; `unsent` is replaced as a whole.
- The client task never blocks, and with no subscribers there is no deadlock.
- A healthy quiet connection is kept.
Not attacked with a test: an INFO update that lowers `max_payload` between the check and the write (every client has this race, and nats-server sends full INFOs).
