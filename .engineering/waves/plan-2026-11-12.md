# Wave plan 2026-11-12

The order of the accepted stories that are not started, after release v0.16.2. It replaces rows 9 to
12 of `plan-2026-11-07.md`. The order is the coordinator's (decision of 2026-11-12 on the store
audit: store upkeep and the order are the coordinator's; no quality row is waived; a change of the
language's architecture comes back as a question before work starts).

Two lanes run side by side, one unit each. Every unit of lane G changes
`crates/ignite-rustgen/src/lib.rs` in the ownership rules, so lane G is serial. Lane L changes the
checker, both backends and std in other places; a unit of lane L merges `main` before its gate.
